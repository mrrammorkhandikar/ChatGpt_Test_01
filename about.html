About

A flexible team for serious digital work.

Design, engineering, AI, mobile, desktop and delivery — assembled around the product, not a fixed agency package.

Shriram Morkhandikar.

Software engineer and AI / GenAI practitioner working across web applications, APIs, machine learning, LLM products, RAG, agentic workflows and full-stack delivery.

The team stays lean for a focused website, or expands with design, frontend, backend, mobile, AI, QA and deployment specialists when the product needs it.

That range is practical. A portal may only need a web build. An assistant over internal documents needs retrieval, access control and a place for the answer to land. A field workflow may need the same account on the phone. The shape of the team follows that, instead of a standing bench billed in advance.
