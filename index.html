How we work

From first sketch to production.

Four visible stages, with room to adjust when the product teaches us something new.

  1. 01

    Explore & Plan

    Users, the current workflow, the outcome, and a practical scope.

  2. 02

    Design & Prototype

    Flows and screens you can click through while changes are still cheap.

  3. 03

    Develop & Test

    Short cycles. Roles, data, edge cases and the paths people actually use.

  4. 04

    Deploy & Support

    Environment, handover, launch, and support after the first release.
