Patterns that repeat

Most products are a queue, a record, and a role.

A queue

Appointments, matters, orders, leads, drafts. Someone needs to see what is waiting.

A record

The document, the patient slot, the property, the invoice. The product has to keep it findable.

A role

Staff, client, admin, reviewer. Access is part of the design, not a setting added at the end.
