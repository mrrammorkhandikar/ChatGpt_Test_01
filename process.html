Process

Structured enough to be reliable. Flexible enough to be human.

Every project is different, but the delivery rhythm stays understandable: discover, plan, design, build, test, launch and support.

01

Discover

Understand the business, users, current system, constraints and outcome.

02

Plan

Break the product into milestones, priorities, architecture and responsibilities.

03

Design

Create user flows, screens, states, responsive UI and acceptance criteria.

04

Build

Develop frontend, backend, database, integrations and mobile/desktop clients.

05

Test

Validate functionality, responsive behaviour, permissions and edge cases.

06

Launch

Configure hosting, domains, analytics, environments and documentation.

07

Support

Maintain, measure, fix, improve and add capabilities after launch.

Engagement models

Choose how you want to work.

01

Fixed scope

Defined website, dashboard or focused application.

02

MVP sprint

Smallest useful version, release, learn and iterate.

03

Dedicated team

Flexible design, frontend, backend, mobile, AI and QA capacity.

04

Maintenance

Existing product support, upgrades, fixes and new features.
